I am a Senior Research Scientist at Adobe Research. I received my Ph.D. from Yale University, advised by [Prof. Holly Rushmeier](https://graphics.cs.yale.edu/people/holly-rushmeier) and [Prof. Julie Dorsey](https://graphics.cs.yale.edu/people/julie-dorsey). Prior to Yale, I received my bachelor’s degree from Zhejiang University, where I was advised by [Prof. Rui Wang](http://www.cad.zju.edu.cn/home/rwang/).

My research focuses on 2D and 3D graphics, with a particular interest in generative AI for spatial content creation. At Adobe, we bring research prototypes into real-world creative workflows, with technologies shipped in [Adobe Substance 3D](https://helpx.adobe.com/substance-3d-stager/desktop/release-notes/version-3-1-0.html), [Firefly Services](https://developer.adobe.com/firefly-services/docs/s3dapi/guides/generate-3-d-object-composite/), [Firefly Boards](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/create-mood-boards/firefly-boards/convert-2d-images-into-3d-assets.html), and [Project Aqua](https://aqua.adobe.com/). Our work spans generative 3D asset creation, image composition, and spatial content workflows. Most recently, our research combining generative 3D AI with image editing led to Photoshop’s [Rotate Object](https://research.adobe.com/news/new-photoshop-feature-rotate-2d-objects-in-three-dimensions/), enabling precise 3D-aware control of objects in 2D images. Our generative models have also been deployed in enterprise workflows, including Adobe’s [collaboration with Disney Imagineering](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/06/adobe-and-disney-imagineering-collaborate).

I am currently exploring generative world models and agentic workflows for visual content creation. I am open to collaborations and welcome strong Ph.D. students interested in research internships at Adobe Research.
